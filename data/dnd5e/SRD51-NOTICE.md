`srd51-spell-facts.js` extracts the 319 spell headers and eight base class lists directly from this PDF (printed pages 105–194). Regenerate with `scripts/extract-srd51-spells.py` and a local copy of that PDF. English spell names are identity keys. Classes supplied by subclasses are deliberately excluded from the base class lists.

`scripts/grimoire-rules.js` contains project translations and editorial names derived from the open English SRD. These are **not** an official Russian translation. Eight complete descriptions have been reviewed in this revision; the remaining Russian descriptions retain the previous Open5e machine translations and are explicitly marked as awaiting full review. The two existing PHB-only entries retain separately identified project descriptions.

The original Open5e snapshots and their own notices remain unchanged as source provenance. The active grimoire derives only one 2014 record for each identity. Its 2024 and third-party variants are not active spell choices.
